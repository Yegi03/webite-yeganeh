There's something satisfying about owning every line of your personal site. No drag-and-drop limits, no template you share with thousands of others.

I'm starting simple: home, about, research, and a blog. Later I'll add search, a newsletter, maybe even an AI assistant trained on my writing.

For now, the goal is clear: **ship something real at [theyeganeh.com](https://theyeganeh.com)** and iterate from there.
